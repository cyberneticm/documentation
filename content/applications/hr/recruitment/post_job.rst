==================
Post job positions
==================

Once a job position has been :doc:`cretaed and configured <new_job>`, the next step is to share the
job position, so that prospective applicants can apply.

Job positions can be shared on the company website, on job boards, or

.. _post-job/website:

Publish to website
==================

To pubish a job listing on the conpany's website, open the **Recruitment** app, and find the desired
job position on the dashboard. A toggle appears in the lower-left corner of every job position card,
and displays either :guilabel:`Not Publused` or :guilabel:`Published`.

Click on the toggle to change the current state of the job position.

When a job position is published, a green :guilabel:`PUBLISHED` banner appears in the topr-right
corner of the card.

To view the listing, click the :icon:`oi-launch` :guilabel:`Job Page` in the lower-right corner of
the job card.
